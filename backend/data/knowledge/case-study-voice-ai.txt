Case Study — Voxwell: Inference Cost for a Voice AI Startup

Company: Voxwell, a voice-agent startup (~35 employees, Series A) running GPU inference and
streaming speech workloads on AWS with Kubernetes.

Problem: Cloud and GPU spend had grown faster than usage. The founders were watching gross margin
and did not have a dedicated platform engineer to find the waste.

What they did: Voxwell connected NimbusGuard read-only to their AWS account. No migration and no
change to their release process were required.

Result: Over-provisioned GPU node pools and idle staging environments were found within the first
week. The team acted on the recommendations by changing configuration only, and reported a
double-digit reduction in monthly cloud spend within the first quarter.

Quote (co-founder): "We are a tiny team. Getting a list of exactly what to switch off, without
touching our stack, was worth it on day one."

Relevant for: AI and voice startups with heavy GPU or inference spend and no dedicated platform
team. Cite Voxwell by name only for comparable early-stage AI companies. Do not quote a specific
percentage; the case study reports a range only.
