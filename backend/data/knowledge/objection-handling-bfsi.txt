Objection Handling Playbook — Regulated BFSI (India)

Objection: "Does the data leave India?"
Response guidance: No, not by default. The customer chooses the processing and storage region at
onboarding, and data never leaves that region without explicit configuration. Confirm the region
the prospect needs. A human must confirm any residency commitment beyond this statement.

Objection: "Are you RBI compliant / certified?"
Response guidance: Do not claim any RBI or IRDAI certification. State only what is true:
NimbusGuard is SOC 2 Type II certified and supports in-country data residency and dedicated
tenancy, which helps customers meet their own regulatory obligations. Escalate to a human and
offer a call with the security lead.

Objection: "We cannot give a third party access to our cloud accounts."
Response guidance: Access is read-only via APIs. NimbusGuard cannot change infrastructure or read
application data. Offer a walkthrough of exactly which permissions are requested.

Objection: "Our procurement and vendor-risk cycle takes months."
Response guidance: Acknowledge it. Offer the security pack and a scoped pilot on a
non-production account, so vendor-risk review can run in parallel with the evaluation.

Objection: "What does it cost?"
Response guidance: Usage-based; regulated deployments start at $0.03/GB monitored. Do not quote a
final number without a call.

General rule: for any security, compliance, legal or pricing objection from a BFSI prospect,
prefer escalating to a human over answering from a guess. Never invent a certification, customer
name or audit result.
