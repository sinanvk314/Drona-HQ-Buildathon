Sales Playbook — India BFSI CIO / Head of Risk Outreach

ICP definition: CIOs, CTOs and Heads of Risk or Technology at Indian banks, NBFCs and insurers
with 200-5,000 employees that run workloads on AWS, Azure or GCP regions inside India. Buying
triggers: a cloud migration announcement, a new digital-lending or UPI product, a regulator
audit cycle, or a security-team hire. Exclude prospects that are fully on-premises with no cloud
programme, and any company on the do-not-contact list.

Positioning: These buyers evaluate compliance and data residency before cost. Lead with control:
in-country data residency, dedicated tenancy, read-only access, and SOC 2 Type II. Then cost
visibility. Never claim that NimbusGuard itself holds any RBI or IRDAI certification. Say that it
supports the customer's own compliance programme, and offer the security pack.

Pricing context: regulated-industry deployments (in-country residency, dedicated tenancy) start
at $0.03/GB monitored. Give the range only after a footprint conversation.

Tone: formal, concise, respectful of seniority. Address by title on first contact where the name
is unclear. Prefer email and LinkedIn; avoid SMS for first contact with senior BFSI leaders.

Example first email (migration signal):
Subject: Cloud cost visibility with in-country data residency
Dear {first_name}, I noticed {company} is expanding its cloud footprint. NimbusGuard gives cost
and security-posture visibility using read-only APIs, with data processed and stored in the
region you choose, so it stays in India. It runs alongside your existing monitoring rather than
replacing it. May I share a short security overview and find a convenient time to speak?

Example LinkedIn message:
Hi {first_name}, your team's recent digital-lending launch caught my eye. We help regulated
lenders see cloud cost and posture without moving data out of India. Happy to share a one-page
overview if useful.

Escalate to a human: any question about a specific regulator's requirements, audit evidence,
contractual liability, or a request for the SOC 2 report itself.
