Case Study — Kaveri Finserv: Cloud Visibility Without Moving Data

Company: Kaveri Finserv, a mid-size NBFC (~1,800 employees) with a digital-lending platform on
AWS in the Mumbai region and Datadog for monitoring.

Problem: Cloud spend on the lending platform had grown faster than loan volume, and the risk team
needed evidence of configuration posture for an upcoming internal audit. Any tool had to keep
data inside India and get through the vendor-risk process.

What they did: Kaveri deployed NimbusGuard in a dedicated-tenancy, in-country configuration with
read-only access to a single AWS account first. Findings appeared inside their existing Datadog
dashboards.

Result: Idle database replicas and over-provisioned node pools were identified in the first three
weeks, and posture findings were mapped to the checks their risk team already tracked. The
vendor-risk review ran in parallel with the pilot, so procurement did not delay the start.

Relevant for: NBFCs, banks and insurers running regulated workloads on AWS, Azure or GCP in
India. Cite Kaveri Finserv by name only for comparable regulated lenders, and never imply a
regulator endorsed the deployment.
