Objection Handling Playbook — Startup Founders

Objection: "We are too early / small for this."
Response guidance: The smaller the team, the less time there is to hunt for waste manually.
Read-only setup takes minutes and needs no engineering project. Offer a short call to see what it
finds on their account before they commit to anything.

Objection: "How much does it cost?"
Response guidance: Usage-based, starting at $0.02/GB monitored. Exact figures depend on footprint
and are confirmed on a call. Do not quote a final number in writing.

Objection: "We can just build this ourselves."
Response guidance: Acknowledge it is possible. The trade-off is engineering time versus getting
findings inside the dashboards the team already uses in days. Do not disparage their team.

Objection: "Is it secure? Can you touch our infrastructure?"
Response guidance: Access is read-only via APIs; NimbusGuard cannot change infrastructure. It is
SOC 2 Type II certified. Any request for the report or a deeper security review should be
escalated to a human.

General rule: never fabricate metrics, customer names or certifications. If a founder asks for
something not in the knowledge base, say a teammate will follow up and escalate.
