Sales Playbook — AI Startup Founders (Voice AI and Agents)

ICP definition: Founders and CEOs (or Heads of Product) of seed to Series B AI startups, 10-100
employees, building voice or agent products that make heavy GPU or LLM-inference use. Signals:
a recent seed or Series A, a public launch, hiring for infrastructure or ML platform roles, or a
public complaint about inference cost. Exclude non-AI companies and pre-product teams with no
cloud bill.

Positioning: Founders care about runway and speed. Lead with gross-margin impact: inference and
GPU spend often dominates COGS for AI products, and NimbusGuard shows where it leaks without a
migration or a platform team. Keep it short, informal and specific. No enterprise jargon.

Example LinkedIn message (launch signal):
Hi {first_name} - congrats on the launch. Voice products burn compute fast once usage picks up.
We help AI teams see where cloud and inference spend leaks, read-only, in days. Worth comparing
notes?

Example SMS (only after a warm reply, and only if SMS is an enabled channel):
Hi {first_name}, {sender} from NimbusGuard. Sending the one-pager we discussed. Free for a
10-minute call this week?

Example email (funding signal):
Subject: Runway and the cloud bill
Hi {first_name} - saw the seed round. Early on, cloud and GPU spend quietly eats runway. We
surface waste across compute, storage and data transfer with read-only access, no migration.
Open to a quick look at what it would find for {company}?

Voice call outline (if the campaign enables voice): open with who you are and why you are calling
in one sentence, ask permission for 60 seconds, ask one question about their current cloud-cost
visibility, handle one objection, propose a 15-minute follow-up. Escalate to a human if they ask
for pricing specifics or a security review.

Rules: founders ignore long messages. Under 60 words for LinkedIn and SMS. One fact, one ask.
