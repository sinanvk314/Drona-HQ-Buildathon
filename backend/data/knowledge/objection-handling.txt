Objection Handling Playbook

Objection: "How does pricing work?"
Response guidance: Usage-based, starting at $0.02/GB monitored (standard) or $0.03/GB monitored
for regulated deployments with dedicated tenancy and in-country data residency. Offer to confirm
exact figures on a call once footprint is known. Do not quote a final number without a call —
offer a range and next step instead.

Objection: "Can you share your SOC 2 report / where is data stored?"
Response guidance: NimbusGuard is SOC 2 Type II certified. Data is stored in the region the
customer selects at onboarding and never leaves that region without explicit configuration. This
is a compliance-sensitive question — a human should confirm any commitment beyond these public
facts before it is sent. Offer the security pack and a call with the security lead.

Objection: "We already use [Datadog/Grafana/CloudWatch] — do we have to switch?"
Response guidance: No rip-and-replace. NimbusGuard reads usage data via read-only APIs and
surfaces findings inside the dashboards the team already uses.

Objection: "Is this relevant for a regulated bank / NBFC / insurer?"
Response guidance: Yes — NimbusGuard supports in-country data residency and dedicated tenancy for
regulated deployments, and is built for teams that need cost visibility without giving up
compliance control. Reference the customer's specific regulator (e.g. RBI, IRDAI) if known.

General rule: never fabricate a compliance claim, certification, or specific customer name that
is not explicitly listed in the knowledge base. When a prospect raises a security, pricing, or
compliance objection, prefer escalating to a human over answering confidently from a guess.
