NimbusGuard — Product One-Pager

What it is: NimbusGuard is a cloud cost and security layer that sits alongside a company's
existing infrastructure (AWS, GCP or Azure) and existing observability stack (Datadog, Grafana,
CloudWatch). It does not require a rip-and-replace migration — it reads usage and configuration
data via read-only APIs and surfaces savings and risk findings directly inside the dashboards
teams already use.

Core capabilities:
- Continuous cloud cost anomaly detection across compute, storage and data transfer.
- Automated right-sizing recommendations for Kubernetes workloads and managed databases.
- Security posture checks mapped to SOC 2 and common regulatory frameworks.
- Data residency controls: customers choose the region their data is processed and stored in.

Pricing: usage-based, starting at $0.02/GB monitored, with volume tiers for larger deployments.
Regulated-industry deployments (in-country data residency, dedicated tenancy) start at $0.03/GB
monitored. Exact figures are confirmed with the account team based on footprint.

Compliance: NimbusGuard is SOC 2 Type II certified. Data is stored in the region the customer
selects at onboarding, and never leaves that region without explicit configuration.

Integrations: AWS, GCP, Azure, Kubernetes, Datadog, Grafana, CloudWatch, Terraform, Slack.

Who it's for: engineering and infrastructure teams at growth-stage SaaS companies, and
technology/risk leaders at regulated enterprises (banks, NBFCs, insurers) who need cost
visibility without giving up compliance control.
